# Taka Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Taka Tracker does not collect, store or share any personal information.

## What the extension does
- Fetches public exchange-rate data from Bangladesh Bank (www.bb.org.bd).
- Fetches public reference exchange rates from the Frankfurter API (api.frankfurter.dev) to draw a 7-day trend chart. These requests contain only currency codes and dates.

## Data stored on your device
The extension caches the fetched rates in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) for a few minutes to speed up loading. This data is public exchange-rate data, stays on your device, and is never sent to us.

## Third parties
Requests to the sources above are made directly from your browser. Like any website, those servers may see your IP address. We do not control their logs. We do not run our own servers, and we use no analytics, advertising, or tracking.
